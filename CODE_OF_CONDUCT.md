# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want this project to be a respectful, constructive, and welcoming place for contributors with different backgrounds, experience levels, disciplines, and viewpoints.

## Expected behavior

Contributors should:

- discuss ideas and technical decisions respectfully;
- critique work rather than people;
- give actionable feedback in good faith;
- acknowledge uncertainty and correct mistakes transparently;
- respect privacy, confidentiality, and applicable intellectual-property rights;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, or deliberately disruptive behavior.

## Scope

These expectations apply to repository discussions, issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project-managed community spaces.

## Reporting

If you experience or observe unacceptable behavior, contact the repository maintainer privately rather than escalating sensitive reports in a public issue. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or take other proportionate action when necessary.
