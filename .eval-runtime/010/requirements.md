# Requirements

- FR-001 — Customers can browse available products.
- FR-002 — Customers can add and remove products from a cart.
- FR-003 — Customers can begin and complete checkout without creating or signing into an account; account creation remains optional.
- FR-004 — Customers can enter the delivery and contact information required to complete checkout regardless of account status.
- FR-005 — Customers can submit an order after required checkout information is complete.
- FR-006 — The system creates an order confirmation after successful order submission.
- FR-007 — Store administrators can view submitted orders.

## Impact / open questions

- Confirm whether guest contact information may be retained beyond what is required for order fulfillment and support.
- Confirm whether optional account creation can occur after order submission and whether an existing guest order may later be linked to an account.
- Review any communications or saved-customer-data behavior that previously assumed an authenticated customer.
